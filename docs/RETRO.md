# Sprint 3 and 4 retro (S3-4, S4-5)

## Kept

- Numbers with their measurement attached. The forecast publishes its MAE; the
  evaluation set publishes its own weakness.
- The risk report was not a formality: it found a compliance hole we would have
  shipped as "done".
- Small, single-purpose modules. Every sprint item landed as one file plus one test.

## Dropped

- We did not deploy the API. No Render account existed, so we published the console
  and left a blueprint instead of pretending.
- No backup demo video was recorded; the console is the backup.

## Changed

- Reviews now happen after the risk audit, not before. The audit changes what "done"
  means.
- Superpowers of the lexicon approach are overstated. Its misses on sarcasm and
  implicit intent pushed us toward explicit, testable rules.
