How it decides

  

    
Classify
Weighted keyword lexicons per department. The highest score wins; clinical equipment (telemetry, ventilators, ECG) counts as IT & Medical Tech.

    
Prioritise
Clinical setting +3, clinical equipment +3, safety hazard +4, urgency +2, routine admin −2. Score ≥5 High, 2–4 Medium, below 2 Low.

    
Structure
Seven-key JSON: ticket_id, timestamp, sender, category, priority, summary, assigned_to. Persisted to a local database file.
