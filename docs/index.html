Hospital operations · MedOps AI Platform

MedOps AI — classify → prioritise → learn from the week

One page, three steps: log a staff request and get a classified, prioritised ticket with a drafted reply; every ticket is stored with its timestamps and response times; the dashboard and the weekly summary are computed from that stored data, not typed in by hand.

Log a staff request

Paste it the way it arrives: an email, a WhatsApp, a note at the ward station. The classifier reads the wording, not just keywords, and tells you how sure it is.

Nothing is sent anywhere: the model runs in this page.
